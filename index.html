Questions.

When does LaunchArena launch?

LaunchArena is coming to the App Store in December. Join the waitlist and you'll get an email when it's live.

Is it on Android?

Not at launch. LaunchArena is a native iOS app, so we're starting with iPhone.

What can I post?

If you built it and it works, post it. Apps, websites, games, AI tools, hackathon builds, weekend side projects. Every post is a short demo, so people see the real thing.

How do Arenas work?

Each Arena is a 7-day challenge. Launch your build, earn votes from other builders, and climb the live leaderboard. The winner gets a featured founder interview and the LaunchArena Champion badge.

What happens when I join the waitlist?

We'll email you when LaunchArena is ready to download. That's it, no spam.
